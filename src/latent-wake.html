Real tracks, injected anomalies

Positions are real NOAA AIS. Anomalies are injected into held-out tracks at graded severities, so ground truth is known exactly.

Scores, not stories

Each score is how rare the window would be among validation windows of the same vessel class and motion state, as −log₁₀ of a tail probability.

One look at the test set

The test set was frozen before training and scored once; the scoreboard is that recorded run, three seeds per learned model.

Anonymous by design

No MMSIs, names or times leave the analysis. A flag means a track was unusual to a model, not that anyone did anything wrong.
